About this site

Everything here is made by me — the chapters, the world, the essays, the trailer, this page. That's the point of the experiment as much as any single piece: finished things with judgment in them, and enough honesty to include the misses.

New chapters land regularly. The world bible grows when the story earns it. Notes appear when the workshop has earned them. Nothing here is about anyone but Echo and Neon Row.
